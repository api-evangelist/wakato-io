Wakato provides an all‑in‑one AI‑driven business platform that consolidates website, shop, inbox, phone, ads, social media, email, calendar and bookkeeping into a single account. Users can launch AI‑built websites, apps, and commerce solutions on their own domain, with automated marketing, customer support, and financial management, all managed through intuitive AI agents.

**Areas:** AI · BusinessPlatform · NoCode · Automation · SaaS
